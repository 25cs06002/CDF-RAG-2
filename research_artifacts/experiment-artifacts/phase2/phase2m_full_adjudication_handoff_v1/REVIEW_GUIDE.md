# Phase 2M — Independent Adjudication Guide

This package contains AI-assisted candidate reviews, not medical gold.

## Reviewer instructions

1. Independently inspect the original publication and passage.
2. Verify the exact cause node, intermediate and effect node.
3. Classify CAUSES, RISK_FACTOR_FOR or ASSOCIATED_WITH
   according to the evidence.
4. Record whether the source supports the complete claim.
5. Preserve uncertainty and distinguish association from causation.
6. Record reviewer identity, date, evidence and decision.
7. Do not copy AI-assisted decisions as independent confirmation.
