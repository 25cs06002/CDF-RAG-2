# CDF-RAG-2 Research Artifacts

This directory stores reproducibility artifacts for the M.Tech thesis:

Adaptive Multi-Round RAG Architecture for Reliable
Domain-Specific Question Answering.

## Directory Structure

### experiment-artifacts/

Experimental outputs, source-review worksheets, evidence
registries, audit reports, and versioned review snapshots.

### experiment-artifacts/phase2/

Medical QA benchmark development and evidence-review artifacts,
including Phase 2K, Phase 2L, and Phase 2M.

### graph-G0/

Preserved original G0 knowledge-graph snapshot and associated
reproducibility files.

## Evidence Status

The Phase 2K-2M medical evidence reviews contain AI-assisted
candidate assessments and independent-adjudication handoff files.

They are not independently validated medical gold labels.

Do not promote candidate relationships into medical gold without
appropriate independent review.

## Reproducibility

Original artifacts should remain unchanged.

New review decisions and experimental results must be saved in
separate versioned directories.

Where SHA256SUMS.txt is present, verify file integrity using:

    sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt

## Publication

Do not publish credentials, private medical information,
restricted documents, or other sensitive materials.
